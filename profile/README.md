# Robotic Haircutting

### Robotic Haircutting Research @ University of Oulu

**Our mission is to make haircutting robots safe, precise, and trustworthy enough to serve real people.** A haircut is not just a styling task. It is close physical interaction with a moving person, using a sharp tool on soft, deformable hair, millimeters from the scalp, ears, and face. We study this problem from several angles. We map the field, reviewing its history, methods, and open challenges. We explore how haircutting robots should be built, drawing on CNC-style precision machining and on mobile robotics. We treat safety as the first requirement, not an afterthought. We are furthermore keen to give these robots intelligence through vision-language-action models. This includes methods and tools for hair and head modeling, hair simulation, perception, motion planning, and evaluation.

We are a research team at the University of Oulu, Finland, led by [Professor Shuai Li](https://www.oulu.fi/en/researchers/shuai-li). Our team brings together researchers in robotics, control, perception, and artificial intelligence, and we work closely with students and international collaborators to move robotic haircutting from research toward real-world use.
